Install Sotto

  1. Open readsotto.app in Chrome or Safari.
  2. Look for an install option in the browser's menu or address bar ("Install app" or "Add to Home Screen").
  3. Open Sotto from where it installed.
  1. Open readsotto.app in Safari. Tap More, then Share (or tap Share directly if it is visible).
  2. Scroll to "Add to Home Screen." Turn on "Open as Web App" if it appears, then tap "Add."
  3. Open Sotto from your Home Screen.
  1. In Chrome, open the menu (⋮).
  2. Tap "Install app."
  3. Open Sotto from your home screen or app drawer.
  1. In Chrome, click the install icon in the address bar (or the menu, then "Install Sotto").
  2. Confirm "Install."
  3. Sotto opens as its own window.

A book you have opened keeps working offline.
