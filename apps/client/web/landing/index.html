Sotto reads with you.

Sotto is a free graded reader. You pick a language and a level, open a book, and read. Tap any word for a translation, or press play and hear the story narrated. When you want to talk about what you read, a voice tutor listens and answers, using your own key or the plan. Nothing is recorded. Everything runs in your browser, and if you would rather run it on your own machine, you can self-host it.

The underlined words

graded reader
A book rewritten at your level. Sotto's books are machine-adapted drafts, and their levels are estimates.
narrated
Every story has audio. The words fill in as the narrator reaches them, and single words have their own pronunciation.
voice tutor
Talk about the passage. It can read to you, explain grammar, quiz your pronunciation, or just discuss the story.
your own key
Paste an OpenAI key in Settings. The page calls OpenAI directly from your browser; the key stays on your device and OpenAI bills you, not us.
plan
The hosted tutor, with nothing to set up: subscribe and it is on. The lighter path is your own key.
self-host
docker compose up serves the app and the tutor from one origin on your own hardware, with local models or your own key.
