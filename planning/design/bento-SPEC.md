# readsotto.app in one screen — spec

**Fit read.** Job: get the whole product in one screen, for a stranger who has 20 seconds or for Noel checking that every device still reads; desktop first, phone as a stack; warm launch temperature. So: a light bento on the app's own paper, single committed mode.

**Register.** Bento: a mixed-span grid where every cell carries a different real artifact. At least three artifact types; no cell is icon-and-caption. Clean rotation after drafting sheet / almanac / specimen.

**Anchor.** Straumvik, bento-grid-web-9 (kombai-gallery s.3 bento notes), adapted: its light paper becomes Sotto canvas #F4ECDF with cells on surface #FBF6EC; its hand-drawn dotted diagrams become the app's own devices (speech fill, marker band, peach selection, the cutout); its one accent becomes Sotto coral with one job (the listening dot and the playhead thumb); the pip-install code cell becomes docker compose and the export document.

**Flood.** Sotto canvas #F4ECDF, surface #FBF6EC, ink #221E1B, ink-2 #6E6459, quiet #B5AB9F, peach #F2C8B4, mark #FFD8A8. One inverted cell in ink.

**Device.** The narration scrub: the opening of Le Chat botté as word tokens; a range input (the playhead) and a play ring drive speech fill, quiet to ink word by word, with a real elapsed readout. One saved word carries the marker band, one tapped word the 55 percent peach fill and feeds the gloss cell.

**Artifacts.** Passage (device), the real fr-chat-botte cover SVG with the cutout, the gloss with its nine-language glossary row, a stat, an inverted statement, code, the tutor dialogue, the locale table. Eight cells, eight types.

**Type cast.** Fraunces 300/400 embedded for wordmark, passage, gloss, stat. Inter/system for UI. System mono 11px .08em caps for metadata.

**Grid.** 12 columns at 1280, rows sized to fill the viewport minus the header; spans 5/3/4 on the top band and 3/3/3/3 below; 16px gaps; single column under 900. Radius {2 covers, 10 cells, pill ring}.

**Ban list.** No icon-and-caption cells. No third colour. No blurred shadows (the cutout only). No cell without a real artifact.

**Output.** `~/Claude/sotto/planning/design/bento.html`.

**Done when.** cleo_verify full run 0 FAIL, WARNs named (hard offsets are the product's own elevation); screenshots read; scrub and play exercised; ledger row + archive entry.
