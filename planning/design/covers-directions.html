Direction fragment: eight of the forty books, drawn by the wave, not a finished comp.

Commits to

  • Instant recognition at 104px
  • One drawn thing per book, cheap to hold in memory

Trades away

  • No atmosphere; a few stories, like Larra's, lack an obvious object

Right when

Right when the shelf needs to read fast, not linger.

Build DNA

AnchorPenguin Great Ideas / Pelican, cut paper
GroundOne ground tone per book, picked from its story, never by hash
DeviceSingle emblem, 2 to 3 tones, upper two thirds; ground text zone below y 232
TypeFraunces 300 title, tracked uppercase author, mono level stamp, as Cover.tsx renders today
CostCheapest to hold in memory: one considered object per book, 40 emblems total

Taken all the way

Every shelf becomes a cabinet of forty small held things.

Direction fragment: eight of the forty books, drawn by the wave, not a finished comp.

Commits to

  • Atmosphere, era and weather in a glance
  • One clear stage and one actor per scene

Trades away

  • Busiest direction at 104px; 40 scenes is the most authoring of the three

Right when

Right when opening a book should feel like arriving somewhere.

Build DNA

AnchorFolio Society flat-scene jackets, Charley Harper geometry
GroundNo separate ground tone; the horizon line is the ground
DeviceFull-bleed flat scene, 4 tones, horizon or stage, one actor; solid text band y 250 to 330
TypeFraunces 300 title set on the solid band, mono level stamp, small caps author
CostMost authoring: 40 distinct scenes, each needing its own weather and stage

Taken all the way

The shelf becomes forty small windows onto forty places.

Direction fragment: eight of the forty books, drawn by the wave, not a finished comp.

Commits to

  • Individuality at scale; works for abstract and CJK titles alike
  • Cheapest to author: one motif tiled, not one scene composed

Trades away

  • Motif reads as plain texture at 104px; the label shape repeats on every book

Right when

Right when 40 books need to ship fast without looking hashed.

Build DNA

AnchorCoralie Bickford-Smith Clothbound Classics
GroundMotif tile repeated full bleed; no separate ground layer beneath it
DeviceOne motif tile, 2 to 3 tones, repeated full bleed; paper label x 24 to 196, y 210 to 300
TypeFraunces 300 title on the label, ink text only; the label is always the light tone
CostCheapest to author: one tile pattern per book, no scene composition

Taken all the way

The shelf reads as a run of forty bound cloth spines.
