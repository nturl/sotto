Direction A

Paper

This direction says: a book is a thing you hold. Every cover is a paper cutout sitting on the page, and the app is the desk.

Commits to

Tactility, calm, editorial confidence. One cutout shadow used everywhere, disciplined to a single job.

Trades away

Overt playfulness. On a phone the hard shadow needs restraint or it reads as retro pastiche.

Right when

Noel wants adults to feel this is a serious reading habit that happens to be charming.

Build DNA

Anchor: Medium's cream broadsheet discipline crossed with Shade's hard offset shadow. Radii {2, 10}. Serif 300 to 400, never bold. Accent E4572E does exactly two jobs: CTA fill and cutout shadow color.

Taken all the way

The whole app feels like a well set paperback with one coral ribbon.

Direction B

Highlighter

This direction says: learning is marking things up. Color is a highlighter pen you drag over the words.

Commits to

Fun, energy, instant legibility of state. Narration progress is visible as marks left on the page.

Trades away

Calm. Risks reading young, and the heavy rounded display face will fight the serif if used past headings.

Right when

Noel wants the app to feel like a game you play with a pen, and daily return energy matters more than gravitas.

Build DNA

Anchor: Duolingo's sticker buttons crossed with Ramp's highlighter marker device. Radii {12, 9999}. One allowed gradient, on the primary CTA only. The sweep is drawn as a skewed rectangle, 180ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).

Taken all the way

Every screen has one marker stroke and nothing else colored. The sweep is the brand.

Direction C

Voice

This direction says: the tutor is a presence in the text, not a widget above it. The words light up as they are spoken.

Canvas F2F1EC
Ink 1B1D22
2nd 6B7079
Accent 1D6B5F

MonoTutor speaking

Commits to

Premium calm, the tutor as the product, accessibility. State is always a word, never a color guess.

Trades away

Warmth and obvious fun. A cool stone canvas is a harder sell for a cozy books habit, and the library stays quiet.

Right when

Noel wants the voice tutor to be the reason people talk about this app, and is fine letting the library be quiet.

Build DNA

Anchor: Things's restraint as brand crossed with Haptic's scroll word fill, repurposed as speech fill. Radii {0, 8}. No coral anywhere. The tutor bubble is nothing, the fill is everything.

Taken all the way

The whole app is stone and ink with one teal that only ever means spoken.
