03 / Your choice

Four ways to use Sotto.

Start with free reading. For conversation, use the on-device tutor or choose how to power it. Your own key and self-hosting are alternatives to the hosted plan.

01

Free reading

The book, the words, the conversation.

Reading, listening, tap-to-translate, saved vocabulary, and an in-browser tutor are free. The tutor runs on on-device models; nothing leaves the device.

No account is needed to try. Your progress and saved words stay in this browser until you create an account, and an account keeps them.

Try a sample without an account →
02

The plan

Hosted voice tutor. Nothing to set up.

$9.99 a month or $79 a year after a 3-day trial.

With the plan, your voice goes to OpenAI through Sotto's server, which brokers each tutor turn. Sotto does not store transcripts or recordings, only usage minutes and billing.

Sign in for the plan at app.readsotto.app →
03

Your own key

Works with or without the plan.

Paste an OpenAI API key in Settings. The page then calls OpenAI directly from your browser.

The key stays on this device. OpenAI bills you, not Sotto.

Read the own-key guide →
04

Run it yourself

Free on your own hardware.

Run Sotto on your own computer. One command serves the app and the tutor from one origin, using local models or your own key.

docker compose up Follow the self-hosting guide →
04 / Keep it close

Install Sotto.

Sotto is a progressive web app. Add it to your phone or desktop from your browser.

iPhone / iPad · Safari

Tap Share, choose “Add to Home Screen”, open Sotto from the Home Screen.

Android · Chrome

Open the menu, tap “Install app”.

Desktop · Chrome

Click the install icon in the address bar (or menu, “Install Sotto”), confirm Install.
