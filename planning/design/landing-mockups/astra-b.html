01Begin with a passage

The words meet you where you are.

Choose a language and a level, then open a short adapted book. Read it, hear the story narrated, and save words to review later.

The reader

The books are machine-adapted drafts, and their levels are estimates.

02Give the passage a voice

Now you have something to say.

You read a passage. Then you talk about it out loud.

The voice tutor answers. It already knows what you read.

Keep the conversation about the story you just opened. You can start with the free tutor that runs on on-device models in your browser.

The free in-browser tutor keeps everything on the device. A hosted plan, your own key, and self-hosting give you other ways to power the conversation, explained below.

03Put the book within reach

Install it from your browser.

Sotto is a progressive web app for your phone or desktop. Follow the steps for your device.

iPhone / iPad Safari

Tap Share, choose “Add to Home Screen”, open Sotto from the Home Screen.

Android Chrome

Open the menu, tap “Install app”.

Desktop Chrome

Click the install icon in the address bar (or menu, “Install Sotto”), confirm Install.

A book you have opened keeps working offline.

04Choose how it runs

Free to read.
Four ways to use it.

The hosted plan is one option for the voice tutor. Your own key and running Sotto yourself are alternatives to that plan.

  1. Free reading

    Open the book and begin.

    Reading, listening, tap-to-translate, saved vocabulary, and an in-browser tutor are free. The tutor runs on on-device models; nothing leaves the device.

    No account is needed to try. Progress and saved words stay in this browser until you create an account, and an account keeps them.

    Try a sample without an account →

  2. The plan

    A hosted voice tutor with nothing to set up.

    $9.99 a month or $79 a year after a 3-day trial.

    With the plan, your voice goes to OpenAI through Sotto's server, which brokers each tutor turn. Sotto does not store transcripts or recordings, only usage minutes and billing.

    Sign in for the plan at app.readsotto.app →

  3. Your own key

    With or without the plan.

    Paste an OpenAI API key in Settings. The page then calls OpenAI directly from your browser.

    The key stays on this device. OpenAI bills you, not Sotto.

    Set up your own key →

  4. Run it yourself

    Free, on your own computer.

    Use your own hardware. One command serves the app and the tutor from one origin, using local models or your own key.

    docker compose up Follow the self-hosting guide →
