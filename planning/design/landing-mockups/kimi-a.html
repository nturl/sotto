What you do in Sotto

Three steps
01 · Choose

Pick a language and a level

Open a short book that has been adapted — rewritten — to exactly that level, so the text stretches you without stopping you.

02 · Read

Tap, listen, save

Tap any word for its meaning. Hear the story narrated. Save the words you want to keep and review them later.

03 · Talk

Discuss what you just read

The voice tutor talks with you about the passage you just finished — it already knows what you read.

The tutor, in plain words

Read, then speak

You read a passage. Then you talk about it — out loud, in the language you are learning. The tutor listens and answers, and because it has the passage in front of it too, the conversation stays on what you actually just read: the words, the plot, what you think happens next.

Below are the four ways to use Sotto. The first is free reading itself; the other three are different ways to power the voice tutor — alternatives to each other, not tiers.

01 · Free reading

Free reading

Reading, listening, tap-to-translate, saved vocabulary, and an in-browser tutor that runs on on-device models are free — with the on-device tutor, nothing leaves your device. No account is needed to try: your progress and saved words stay in this browser until you create an account, and an account keeps them. Start free or try a sample.

02 · The plan

The plan — a hosted voice tutor

Nothing to set up: sign in and talk. $9.99 a month or $79 a year, after a 3-day trial. Sign-in for the plan is at app.readsotto.app. With the plan, your voice goes to OpenAI through Sotto’s server, which brokers each tutor turn; Sotto does not store transcripts or recordings — only usage minutes and billing.

03 · Your own key

Your own key

Works with or without the plan. Paste an OpenAI API key in Settings and the page calls OpenAI directly from your browser. The key stays on this device, and OpenAI bills you, not Sotto. Read the guide.

04 · Run it yourself

Run it yourself

Free, on your own hardware. docker compose up serves the app and the tutor from one origin, using local models or your own key. Read the self-hosting guide.

Add Sotto to your device

Installs like an app
iPhone / iPad

In Safari, tap Share, choose “Add to Home Screen”, then open Sotto from your Home Screen.

Android

In Chrome, open the menu and tap “Install app”.

Desktop

In Chrome, click the install icon in the address bar (or open the menu and choose “Install Sotto”), then confirm Install.

A book you have opened keeps working offline.

A quiet caveat: the books are machine-adapted drafts, and their levels are estimates.
