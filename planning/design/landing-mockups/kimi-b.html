Sotto Free · Open source · Graded reader

Read at your level. Talk about what you read.

Sotto is a free, open-source graded reader: open a short book rewritten to the level you choose, tap any word for its meaning, hear it narrated — then discuss the passage out loud with a voice tutor that has read the same page.

Chapter 02 · The tutor — read, then speak

You read a passage. Then you talk about it, out loud.

The voice tutor listens and answers — in the language you are learning — about the exact passage you just finished. It already knows what you read, so it can ask what you thought, check a word you stumbled on, or keep the story going.

Chapter 03 · Using Sotto — four ways, no tiers

Reading is free. Power the tutor whichever way suits you.

The plan is one option; your own key and your own hardware are alternatives to it, not lesser versions of it.

Free reading

Start here — free, no account needed to try

Reading, listening, tap-to-translate, saved vocabulary, and an in-browser tutor that runs on on-device models are free; with the on-device tutor, nothing leaves your device. Your progress and saved words stay in this browser until you create an account, and an account keeps them.

The plan

A hosted voice tutor, nothing to set up

$9.99 a month or $79 a year, after a 3-day trial. Sign-in for the plan is at app.readsotto.app. With the plan, your voice goes to OpenAI through Sotto’s server, which brokers each tutor turn; Sotto does not store transcripts or recordings — only usage minutes and billing.

Your own key

Bring an OpenAI API key

Works with or without the plan. Paste the key in Settings and the page calls OpenAI directly from your browser. The key stays on this device, and OpenAI bills you, not Sotto. Read the guide.

Run it yourself

Self-host, free, on your own hardware

One command — docker compose up — serves the app and the tutor from one origin, using local models or your own key. Read the self-hosting guide.

Chapter 04 · Install — keep it on your device

Add Sotto to your phone or desktop.

iPhone / iPad · Safari

Tap Share, choose “Add to Home Screen”, then open Sotto from your Home Screen.

Android · Chrome

Open the menu and tap “Install app”.

Desktop · Chrome

Click the install icon in the address bar (or open the menu and choose “Install Sotto”), then confirm Install.

A book you have opened keeps working offline.

A quiet caveat: the books are machine-adapted drafts, and their levels are estimates.
