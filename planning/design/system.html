Sotto design system

North star: a well-set paperback with one coral ribbon. Books are paper things on a warm desk; the app stays quiet so the text and covers carry the delight.

Color, type, radius, elevation, spacing

02 - Signature devices

Three, each with one job

The cutout press, the marker stroke, and speech fill. Everything else in the app is quiet on purpose.

Device A - the cutout

Press pushes paper flat

Press and hold the button. It translates 2px and the ink shadow shrinks from 4px to 2px, 120ms ease.

Device B - the marker stroke

Saved words carry a mark

Click a word to draw a skewed peach stroke under it, 240ms. Click again to erase it right to left.

Device C - speech fill

Quiet becomes ink as the narrator arrives

Press play. Each word turns from quiet to ink with a 60ms stagger, no highlight box chases the voice.

06 - Don't

Discipline, not decoration

01

No blurred shadows, no glass, no gradients on surfaces except the daily card's teal panel.

02

No accent outside CTA fill and the active tab; the speaker ring uses accent as a 2px outline only.

03

No bold Fraunces. Weight 300 or 400 only.

04

No pill-chip navigation, no orb, no mascot, no flags as language identifiers.

05

No highlight box chasing narration; speech fill only.

06

No inline colors in screens; every value comes from the token file.
