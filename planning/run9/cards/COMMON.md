- e2e selectors in `apps/client/e2e/hosted.mjs`, `voice-live.mjs`, `audible-probe.mjs`, `browser-tutor.mjs` must keep working (same testIDs, same visible text) or be updated in the same commit.

- Done = `planning/run9/<lane>-report.md` in your worktree, committed: what changed (files, SHAs), tests run with output pasted, evidence paths, what is NOT verified and why, requests outside your lane, anything needing Noel. Mark every claim VERIFIED (you saw it) or INFERRED. Two serious attempts at anything, then write the escalation in the report and stop.
